Photographed by Inez van Lamsweerde and Vinoodh Matadin in the Turks and Caicos Islands for the 2014 global launch of Calvin Klein Eternity Night. Actor and filmmaker Ed Burns stars alongside supermodel Christy Turlington in a campaign celebrating the enduring legacy of the Eternity fragrance collection. Set against a tranquil coastal backdrop at twilight, the campaign reinforces Calvin Klein's signature themes of timeless romance, modern elegance, and iconic fragrance advertising.
