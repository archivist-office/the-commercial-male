Calvin Klein

Photographer

Bruce Weber

Models

Photographed by Bruce Weber at an estate in Montecito, California. Shot in 1989 for Calvin Klein Obsession for Men — launched in 1986. Part of Weber's Point Conception series with Arango, later exhibited as fine art photography.
Gianni Versace
1989 · Update Campaign

Photographer

Bruce Weber

Models

  • Deryl George Left
  • Ric Arango Right
Photographed by Bruce Weber for Gianni Versace, 1989. The same year Weber shot Ric Arango for Calvin Klein Obsession — one of the most prolific years in commercial male campaign photography. Deryl George remains one of the most underrecognized major male models of the era.

Jeff Aquilon
1983 · Editorial

Photographer

Bruce Weber

Models

  • Jeff Aquilon Primary
Photographed by Bruce Weber, 1983. Jeff Aquilon is widely regarded as the first male supermodel — a distinction earned through his dominance of the highest commercial tier in global fashion advertising during the late 1970s and early 1980s.
GQ Magazine
1988 · Editorial

Photographer

Piero Gemelli

Models

  • James Vaccaro Left
  • Burke Hudson Right
Photographed by Piero Gemelli for GQ Magazine, 1988. James Vaccaro and Burke Hudson were among the most recognizable American commercial faces of the late 1980s — this editorial captures the era's signature tailored authority.
Axios Man
1997 · Global Campaign

Photographer

Richard Avedon

Location

Los Angeles, CA

Models

  • Brett Salisbury Primary
Photographed by Richard Avedon in Los Angeles, 1997. Brett Salisbury served as the global face of Axios Man by Nexxus — a haircare and grooming line by Alberto-Culver, distributed internationally. One of the most significant commercial male grooming campaigns in the world in 1997.
David Gandy
2018 · Editorial

Photographer

Gustavo López

Models

  • David Gandy Primary
Photographed by Gustavo López, 2018. David Gandy remains the most institutionally dominant commercial male model of the modern era — the definitive culmination of everything the system built toward across five decades.
Maurizio Montani
2023 · Editorial

Photographer

Maurizio Montani

Models

  • Aleksa Gavrilović Primary
Photographed by Maurizio Montani, May 2023. Aleksa Gavrilović is one of Serbia's most prominent international male models, with a career spanning the highest tier of global luxury fashion. Montani is one of Milan's most respected editorial photographers.
Esquire
1994 · Editorial

Photographer

Troy Word

Models

  • John Foster Primary
Photographed by Troy Word for Esquire, 1994. John Foster was one of the most naturally authoritative commercial male faces of his era — the kind of model who made every garment look like it was made for him. A legend of the industry, and deeply missed.
